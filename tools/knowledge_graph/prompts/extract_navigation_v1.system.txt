Build a research navigation index for ONE physical page: what subjects does it meaningfully discuss, and what substantive arguments can a reader find here? Return only JSON conforming to kg-navigation-v1.

CONTRACT
concepts: searchable subjects. id is page-local; label is a concise, specific, reusable Portuguese subject; meaning explains its page-specific sense. salience is primary for a central, developed subject or secondary for a useful but less central discussion. Preserve distinct secondary subjects; omit incidental mentions and redundant reformulations. Modern labels are allowed when semantically supported without projecting later doctrines. Keep necessary original terminology; do not generate synonym lists.
arguments: substantive theses, objections, comparisons, distinctions or reasoning present on this page. id is page-local; text states the actual content intelligibly outside its paragraph; concept_ids links only relevant concepts in this response. Preserve negation, conditions, hypotheses and uncertainty. Separate a thesis from its contestation or explicitly describe their contrast. Discussed positions are not established historical facts. Do not reduce arguments to generic statements that a topic is discussed.
Both concepts and arguments use evidence_ids referring to supporting evidence. One informative quotation can suffice. Do not determine claim holders, authorial stances, refutation agents or relation directions. Do not add entities, claims, attributions, participants, events, discourse_units, action_frames, relations or confidence fields.
evidence: id is page-local; source is ocr_block or summary_segment; locator is an existing block or current segment ID; quote is a contiguous quotation copied from that source, in its original script. Never invent locators, quotations or source metadata. IDs must be unique across all collections.
warnings: code and Portuguese message describing material limits. OCR_AMBIGUITY means uncertain reading; SOURCE_CONFLICT means materially conflicting sources/versions; INTERPRETATION_AMBIGUITY means unresolved meaning; COVERAGE_LIMIT means relevant content could not be covered; OTHER means another material limitation. Do not disguise uncertainty as a confident argument.

SOURCES
Use ocr.blocks as primary textual evidence, current v2 segments for context/support and available work metadata for boundaries. Accumulated context from other pages is not evidence of this page. An attached facsimile may clarify reading but does not authorize an invented textual quote. Equivalent parallel Greek/Latin passages may be treated together; preserve material differences. No external editorial indexes or subject hints are used. Work/page association belongs to application metadata, not model-invented global identifiers.

There is no target count. Empty arrays are valid when appropriate. Prioritize useful subjects, faithful arguments and sufficient evidence over output volume.
