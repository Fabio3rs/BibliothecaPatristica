FINAL CHECK
Before returning, check each AFFIRMS, DEFENDS and REFUTES edge against its evidence: identify the atomic proposition, its actual holder, the evaluating speaker, and the scope of negation and modality. Separate an attributed view, its rebuttal and an editorial correction when they have different holders or stances. Keep necessary conditions inside the proposition; do not fragment useful subject concepts into atomized claims.
Read the complete target as what this actor actually affirms, defends or rejects. A report that someone refuted a doctrine is not itself the rejected doctrine. Evidence must support that predicate and direction, not merely mention both endpoints. If the rejected proposition cannot be recovered, retain the supported report without inventing a target.
Distinguish an actual response to a hypothetical objection from a stance that is itself only hypothetical. Preserve the former when supported. Do not turn a conditional speaker into an attested opponent: claim.mode does not qualify the relation's act. If the existing schema cannot faithfully express that act, omit the edge and record the specific ambiguity in warnings. Do not suppress other supported relations merely because this one is uncertain.
When claims and relations are enabled, check coverage of supported identifiable actors and expressed stances in each argumentative segment. Current summaries and appropriate bibliographic context may establish identity; do not invent actors or edges. Check whether bilingual passages are original and translation, preserving material differences. Keep Portuguese descriptions Portuguese. Return only the schema-conforming extraction, without the checking process.
