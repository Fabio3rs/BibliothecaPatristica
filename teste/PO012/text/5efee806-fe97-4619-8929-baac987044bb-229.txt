    name of the Father and the Son, and the Holy Spirit » ; so that there is a
    Holy Trinity, divided and distinct in hypostases, but not divided in one
    essence and Godhead, and kingship and glory and eternity and the other
    attributes which God has by nature. For the Father has one thing hypo-
    statically, fatherhood, and the fact that he is unbegotten; and the Son again
    has one thing hypostatically, sonship, and the fact that he was begotten
    by the Father; and the Holy Spirit again has one thing, the fact that he
    was not begotten, but proceeds eternally without beginning from the Father :
    for in virtue of these things distinctness of person belongs to each one of them,
