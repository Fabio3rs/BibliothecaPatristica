  <bloco tipo="texto_principal" script="latino" bbox="50,565,880,730">
    But perhaps someone will say that, when the wise Basil said, « Grace
    arising from the dispensation »¹, he referred to the coming in the
    flesh. But let us know clearly that the Word of God, even before he
    rose upon us in fleshly form, constantly controlled² our life and our salvation;
    for 'in him we live and move and exist'³ : for the name 'dispensation'
    is not applied to the grace of the Humanization among us only. And
    Gregory the Theologian⁴ is a witness of this when he says, « The other
    appellations etc. »¹.
