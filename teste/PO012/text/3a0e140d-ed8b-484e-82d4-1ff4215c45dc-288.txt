    business, as far as possible, to bring this dissertation also to certain men

    divine and profane learning, and read it to them, generally stealing an

    that I may seem to have prepared this while I was living in Antioch, lest
    perhaps these adversaries might kindle a greater flame of prejudice against
    me, if they perceived that this had been composed by me in exile³: and in
    truth when I was there I began to prepare material for an answer, though,
    when the persecution came, it scattered these things.
    Since you, the God-loving presbyter Philip³, have often asked for the
    Book of Dispensations as you say to be sent you, the only reason for which
    we have put off doing this is that we are desirous of first carrying out your
    request, and casting an eye of criticism on what is written in it; which
    down to this day we have been confident of being able to do, though all our
    attention was occupied with the said composition. Therefore, when we
    have read your book also hastily, we will send it as soon as possible.
