    And, when the holy Cyril with the holy synod at Ephesus accomplished
    the deprivation² of the evil Nestorius, the bishops of the East, though they
    contended for his rejected tenets³, afterwards agreed to the deprivation² of
    that wolf and the rejections of the hateful tenets³; and there was never

    in the meanwhile, who contended for the wickedness of Nestorius? For
    these things, as I have said, general unions have no room; but they remained
    without examination; since many are in fact passed over at councils, although
    they have often been involved in impious opinions. Since then at the present
    time some common agreement among the churches is hoped for, do not
    lower your mind to untimely hair-splitting. As there is a time to speak and
    a time to be silent⁴, so there is a time both to inquire into a matter of this
