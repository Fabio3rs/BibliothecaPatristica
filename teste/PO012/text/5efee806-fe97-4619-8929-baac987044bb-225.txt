    XIX. — OF THE HOLY SEVERUS, FROM THE LETTER TO ISIDORE, THE BEGIN-
    NING OF WHICH IS, « I rejoiced greatly when I received the letter of
    [your] Christ-loving illustriousness ».
  </bloco>
  <bloco tipo="texto_principal" script="latino" bbox="50,625,950,755">
    The doctors called the Holy Trinity co-essential, that through this word, 508-11(?).
    composed in so polished and very sublime a way, they might express both the
    fact of the oneness of essence¹ and that of the division of hypostases, by the
    same expression both union and division, in one word. For, when the Son is
    said to be co-essential with the Father and the Holy Spirit, he claims to
    share in essence¹ with those who are recounted, but to differs in hypostasis.²
  </bloco>
