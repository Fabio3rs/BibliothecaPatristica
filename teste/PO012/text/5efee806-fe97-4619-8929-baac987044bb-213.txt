  <bloco tipo="texto_principal" script="latino" bbox="50,615,900,775">
    Where then we confess the one out of two, Lord and Son and Christ, and
    one incarnate nature of the Word himself, we understand the difference as it
    were in the natural characteristics of the natures from which Christ is. But,
    if we speak of two natures after the union, which necessarily exist in
    singleness and separately, as if divided into a duality, but united by a
    conjunction of brotherhood (if we ought to call such a thing unity), the
    notion of difference reaches to the extent of division, and does not stop at
    natural characteristics.
