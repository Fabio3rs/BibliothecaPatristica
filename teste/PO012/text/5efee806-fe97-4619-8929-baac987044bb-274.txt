    XXX. — OF THE HOLY SEVERUS, FROM THE LETTER TO THE PRESBYTER
    VICTOR², BECAUSE SOMEONE WHEN READING WITH THE SAME VICTOR IN THE BOOK
    OF THE SAME PATRIARCH SAID TO HIM THAT IT IS NOT PROPER TO SAY THAT THE
    BREAD WHICH IS CONSECRATED UPON THE HOLY ALTARS, WHICH IS THE BODY
    OF EMMANUEL, IS IMPASSIBLE AND IMMORTAL, AND IS A GIVER OF IMPASSI-
    BILITY AND IMMORTALITY TO THOSE WHO PARTAKE OF IT, THOUGH EVEN HE
    HIMSELF SAID AND CONFESSED THAT THE BREAD WHICH HAS BEEN TRANSMUTED
    IS THE BODY, BUT NOT IMPASSIBLE, BECAUSE IT IS BROKEN AND DIVIDED; IN
    ANSWER TO WHICH THE HOLY MAN HIMSELF SPEAKS AS FOLLOWS.
