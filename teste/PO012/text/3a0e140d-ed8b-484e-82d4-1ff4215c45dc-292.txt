    and shaken, what is there to expect, save sore stripes from God, and great
    evils that cannot be assuaged and wrath that is poured out, which he who
    in just judgment brings it declares in such words as these : « Behold! my
    wrath and mine anger is poured out upon this country and upon men and
    upon cattle and upon all things that are in the field, and upon all the fruits
