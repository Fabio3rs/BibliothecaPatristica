A CAP. III. — Quinam ex Cain nati sunt ab origine ac septimam usque generationem.

1 Cain, 2 Enoch, 3 Gaiadad, 4 Maiel, 5 Mathusala, 6 Lamech, 7 Jobel et Jubal et Thobel. Sub his vero omnimodum contigit Cainitarum exterminium.

CAP. IV. — Quinam servati sunt a diluvio in arca.

Noe, et filii tres, Sem, Cham, Japheth, horumque uxores quatuor 7.

CAP. V. — Qui sunt Jacobi filii, patriarchæ populi.

Ex Lia : Rubim, Symeon, Levi, Judas, Issachar, Zabulon. Ex Rachel : Joseph, et Benjamin. Ex Balla : Dan, Nephthalim. Ex Zelpha : Gad, Asser. Isti in LXXV animabus descenderunt in Ægyptum 8.
