    Son and Word of the Father; who takest or tookest away the sin¹ of the
    world²; have pity and mercy upon us. Thou who takest or tookest away the
    sin of the world, incline thine ear to us and receive our petition. Thou who
    sittest³ in glory at thy Father's right hand; have pity and mercy upon us;
    because thou only art holy; thou only art the Lord; Jesus Christ; with
    thy⁴ Holy Spirit; to the praise of God the Father, Amen! Always all⁵ the
    days of my life I will bless thee; and I will praise the holy and blessed name
    for ever; which is and abideth for ever and ever. Blessed art thou, all-ruling
    Lord; God of our fathers; and thy name is praised and glorified with a song
    of praise for ever⁶ and ever, Amen! Thee praise beseemeth; thee a song
    of praise befitteth; thee laud hath befitted; the God of all and Father; the
    Father of truth with the only Son; and the all-Holy Spirit; now and always
    and for ever and ever, Amen!
