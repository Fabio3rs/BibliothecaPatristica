    thee, « Our God is a refuge and strength and our helper in the troubles that
    have prevailed and have found us exceedingly »⁴. Therefore⁵, my Lord,
    forget not thy covenant (διαθήκη), nor remove the great mercy of thy kindness
    from us; and let not the hostile and Assyrian peoples enter⁶ into thine
    inheritance; but exalt thy horn⁷ for the sake of thy holy name; and do to us
    and deal with us according as thou alone art good and merciful.
