    of godliness we receive the life-giving body and blood of God the Word
    who became incarnate, for whose sake they readily poured out the blood of
    their necks, showing that it was on behalf of a great matter that they endured
    great contests and tortures; and of the hope of the glory which they look
    to receive amid the splendours and mansions of the saints this holy mystery is
    the firstfruits and opening pleasure : with whom let us praise Christ, saying,
    « Halleluyah! »
