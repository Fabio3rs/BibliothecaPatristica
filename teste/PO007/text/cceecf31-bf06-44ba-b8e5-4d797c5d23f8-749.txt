    « All ⁴ these things have come upon us and we have not forgotten thee
    nor committed iniquity in thy covenant (διαθήκη). Turn not therefore thy
    face away from us, and forget not our neediness and weakness", and the
    tribulations that surround us. Arise, Lord our God, help us in thy grace.
