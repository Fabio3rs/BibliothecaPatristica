thoughts spring up in men's minds like plants, and incite by enticing us
to evil desires. Therefore I beg thee, Christ, God, do not, my Lord,
lead my wretched heart aside to words of wickedness; lest by performing
acts of lawlessness and by means of foul passions being submerged as
in a cave I be overpowered by the evil one and delivered to a reprobate

be shaken; but bring me back to thee, merciful one, because of thy grace
and thy great mercy.</bloco>
