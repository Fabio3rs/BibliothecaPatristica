    268 — 1 — I. — « Remember me, Lord, with the favour of thy people »⁵.
    — If the city⁶ of the believers is above or in heaven, whence⁷ also⁸ we expect
    to receive a Saviour, the Lord Jesus Christ, then let us not ascribe or impute
    our names and appellations to the passing⁹ days of this age of time; for,
    « our days pass away as a shadow »¹⁰, sings David, but let us by means of
    the performance and due observance of good deeds be prepared¹¹ for the future
