    I am walking in the narrow way² of thy holy commandments, the king or
    chief of the spirits of evil, the arrogant and world-ruling devil, who because
    of his pride fell from³ heaven, in order to catch me for destruction⁴, has
    bent⁵ for me buried snares⁶ by means of the ropes of sins. Break and
    cleave them and deliver me by thy great power, Lord, and direct⁷ my steps
    in thy paths and guide me according to the abundance of thy great mercy.
