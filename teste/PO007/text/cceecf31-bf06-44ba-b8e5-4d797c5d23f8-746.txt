287 -- xv -- VI. « Bring my soul out of prison that I may give
thanks to thy name » ¹. — A valley of weeping² is this world, which buries
and covers my wretched soul in the valley of sin as in a dark grave.
Therefore with tears and groans³ I entreat thee, Christ, God, draw it
with thy almighty arm out of the ditch of wretchedness; and restore it
again to the grace of thy divine image; in order that, seeing the comeliness⁴
beyond telling that is in thy right hand, it may enjoy⁵ and be sated
with satiety that fails not, at the manifestation of⁶ thy glory.
