    the subject of the history of the great blessed man of God our father Kashish

    not think it right even after his decease also to withhold the treasure full of
    spiritual riches of his great modes of life and of his honoured labours. This
    saint therefore was by birth (γένος) from a village called that of ‘Aina da
    Frakha² which is beyond Nirba³ d Škwš, and, while he was still a little
