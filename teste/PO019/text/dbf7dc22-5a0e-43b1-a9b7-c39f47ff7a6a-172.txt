    and set up a bishop in it, a man whose name was Kashish¹ from the monastery of John of Ephesus, both of whom were from the monastery of the house of Mar John at Amida. And again they went back and crossed to the regions of Caria, and there they set up two bishops, one whose name was Paul in a city the name of which is Aphrodisias², and another whose name was Julian in a city the name of which is Alabanda³. These are all the bishops and archbishops, who are in number twenty-seven, besides two patriarchs whom the blessed James ordained with all the other bishops, besides many clergy in all the eastern and western countries. Many of all these were instituted in our presence, and with regard to the truth of all these things we give confident and truthful witness that they were done as above, and we have made a commemorative record of them for the knowledge of posterity; but of the sublime manner of life of the blessed James, and his courage and perfection, we have made a record in the preceding history.
