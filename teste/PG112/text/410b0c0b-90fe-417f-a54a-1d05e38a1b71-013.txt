riore editoris notæ continentur, Paris. 1711, fol. [Rec. Venetiis 1729, 2 voll. fol.] Non minus in his

thesaurum, servare se scripsit Egnatius (f), cujus codex ms. deinde pervenit in bibl. Palatinam, ex quo
