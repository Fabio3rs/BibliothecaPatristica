te spiritualium frugum in mensura propension, ut sis

duris, in risu rarior, suavior in conversatione, gra-
vior in vultu, et gestu et habitu, moderatior in
verbis, profusior in lacrymis, in charitate ferventior.

rationem, vide si diem et noctem sine culpabiliset
