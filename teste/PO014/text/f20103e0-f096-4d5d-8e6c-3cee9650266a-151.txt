    plain words, « Blessed art thou, Simon Bar Jona, for flesh and blood revealed
    it not to thee but my Father which is in heaven »³. And James and John,
    as Mark the Evangelist related, being brothers in the spirit more than in the
    body, were named by our Saviour Bnai ragsh, that is 'sons of thunder'⁴.
