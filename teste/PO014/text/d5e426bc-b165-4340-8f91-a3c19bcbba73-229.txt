    thought to put forth another hypostasis, that of the Son, and similarly further
    conceived the thought of putting forth a third hypostasis also from himself,
    that of the Spirit, in order that he might exist in a Trinity. On this theory
    it will result that the Son and the Holy Spirit are not ever-existing but were
    afterwards added to the Father, and we shall give occasion for the blasphemy
    of Arius who says, « There was once when the Son and the Holy Spirit
    were not ». But this is abominable; for one who was acquired later and
    came into being afterwards is not God; for the Holy Trinity is of equal
    everlastingness, and it is therefore understood and acknowledged to be one
    God truly in three hypostases. The words used by the doctor we should
    therefore understand in the same way as that which is stated in Wisdom,
