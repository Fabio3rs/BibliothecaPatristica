    work; and we ourselves on the 1st day of the week honour the preceding
    night as united with the honoured day, and not the night following. Accor-
    dingly therefore they say that every night-and-day is as one may say one
    entity, and, what therefore anyone does during one of these 24 hours, that
    act is said to have been performed on the whole of that day. As for instance,
    when a man is sent on a long journey and returns to his own country or
    city either on the 2nd or on the 3rd day of the week, this man entered the
    city during one hour or a small portion of an hour, but everyone says, « So-
    and-so arrived on the 2nd day of the week » or « on the 3rd », and they
    reckon the whole day which is one with the preceding night to his arrival.
