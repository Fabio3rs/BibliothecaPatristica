It is told also concerning our holy father that while yet (ἔτι) he stood
singing (ψάλλειν) with the brethren by night, our father Abba Athanasius
the apostolic (ἀποστολικός) appeared unto him and said unto him : « Peace
(εἰρήνη) be unto thee, thou good and faithful servant of God. Peace (εἰρήνη)
unto all thy children and unto them that obey thy laws (νόμος). The sweet
smell of thy prayers (προσευχή) hath mounted up into the presence of God,
as a remembrance for thee for ever, and thy name shall endure to all gene-
rations (γενεὰ) ». And (δέ) when our father Abba Athanasius had said these
things unto him * he was hidden from him. Therefore (οὖν) was the holy * fol. 113 r°.
man glad and he commanded his children that they should name (ὀνομάζειν)
the name of our father Abba Athanasius in the hymn (ὕμνος) of the Three
Holy (ἅγιος) Children; and they do his bidding even unto this day, to the
glory of God.
