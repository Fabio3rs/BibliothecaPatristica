    portion of the day-and-night, and the 1st day of the week as regards the first
    portion, and the sabbath entirely through all the 24 hours, keeping the day
    of the Jews' feast in mourning, that thereby also he might fulfil the prophecy
    spoken through the prophet Amos, which threatens the Jews and says,
    « I will turn your feasts into mourning, and all your songs into wailing »¹.
