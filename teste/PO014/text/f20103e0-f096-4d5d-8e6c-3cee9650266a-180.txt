    Evangelist that he did not undergo the common end, but still continues
    even now to exist without death, like Enoch, of whom the apostle said that
    he 'was translated that he might not see death'², and Elijah of whom it is
    written that he 'was carried up in a whirlwind toward heaven'³. I am
    astonished at the crassness of the ignorance of those who said this and their
    lack of information. As the evangelist manifestly corrects the supposition
    of many and says, « Jesus did not say to him 'He shall not die', but 'If
    I will that he tarry till I come what is it to thee?'»⁴, where is there room
