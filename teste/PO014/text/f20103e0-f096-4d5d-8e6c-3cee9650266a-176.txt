    And David answered and said, « But we have kept away from a woman
    since yesterday, and since the third day. Since I set out on the journey,
    all the slaves have been pure ». And, these things having been so stated
    first, what follows should be read as a question, « Is the journey itself also
    profane? » '; and thereupon the addition will give the reason for which the
    journey is not profane; and the addition runs, « Because it will be hallowed
    to-day by means of my implements », that is to say, 'Will anyone call
    the same journey a profane thing, a journey which has been hallowed by
    means of my implements?', viz 'by means of my armour, and the burden of
    my implements, and by my going to war'. And from this it is manifest
    that, inasmuch as the slaves were accustomed to carry his implements and
    arms, when he went out to war, they were purified. And, that slaves again
