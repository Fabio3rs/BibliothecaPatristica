    'Why that tree was called the tree of good and evil', and 'What is the meaning

    received, for indeed we have been speaking about it for so many days. How
    then did that robber receive them? For God does not promise to bring us
    into Paradise, but into heaven itself; and he did not proclaim a kingdom of
