  <bloco tipo="texto_principal" script="latino" bbox="150,565,950,755">
    John the Evangelist also when he said in his epistle, « Children, it is the
    last season »¹, did not speak falsely. For it is in fact the custom to apply
    the term 'seasons'² not only to those of the days, but also to those of the
    whole year : for the whole course of the year is divided into the spring season,
    the summer season, the autumn season, and the winter season; and the
    winter was in truth that of godlessness, which covered the whole world, in
    which there was all spiritual barrenness, and coldness as regards the fervour
    of the divine Spirit, and the whole earth was completely devoid of the gifts
    from heaven, when Christ 'the Sun of righteousness'³ by his coming in the
    flesh dawned upon us.
  </bloco>
