    which the Romans at the time of the Passion and of the voluntary saving
    Cross sought to clothe our Lord and God Jesus Christ, and indeed did actually

    Matthew related as follows : « And, when they had stripped him, they put

    robes »⁵, and John, « And they covered him with a purple garment »⁶.
