    1. I have, on account of its historical importance, included this Coptic fragment, supplied by Mr. W.E. Crum, by kind permission of the authorities of the Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York, in whose volume on the « Monastery of Epiphanius » it will eventually appear. Mr. Crum states that it is written on a limestone ostrakon, found with many others in a ruined monastery in W. Thebes, during excavations carried out by the New York Museum, and that its date is about 600. The translation is Mr. Crum's, and the subject of the fragment is clearly the events which preceded the expulsion of Macedonius from the see of Constantinople.
