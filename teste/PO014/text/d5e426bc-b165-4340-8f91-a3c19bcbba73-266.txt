    forth and is removed after his entry manifestly cries by the mouth of the
    facts themselves that the mystery, which was previously concealed by means
    of the sacrifices of the law and the shadowy service, and was obscurely
    made known as it were by an indication only, by means of this spiritual
    and rational priestly ministration reveals Christ who is God to those who
    have believed on him.
