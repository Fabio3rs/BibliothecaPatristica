    IV. — NEXT THE FOURTH HISTORY, OF THE SAINTS ABRAHAM AND MARO THE BROTHERS.
  </bloco>
  <bloco tipo="texto_principal" script="latino" bbox="180,610,940,850">
    These [before_ our time and in our time itself gained distinction upon a column and seized the crown of victory through their marvellous ways of life; in whose case also the words found in the Psalms of the Holy Spirit may also aptly be sung : « How good and how beautiful it is for brethren when they dwell together »¹; for to these two holy brethren alike it is good and beautiful and marvellous that they dwell together, as is written in the Psalmist. These two holy brothers were by birth (γένος) from a village called Kalesh², in the territory of Amida; and the elder whose name was Abraham was first moved by zeal, and he went and dwelt in a monastery called Ar'a Rabtha³ in the territory of Ingila.
