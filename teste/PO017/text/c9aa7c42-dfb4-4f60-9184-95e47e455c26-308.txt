    1. See p. 291, n. . — 2. Or 'king and queen'. — 3. This seems to be an exaggerated statement.
    The only satrapies were those of the 5 Armenian territories which Justinian in 536 combined into
    Armenia IV (Novella 31), and that Šnq's post was one of these is confirmed by the fact that his son
    had a hereditary claim to the succession. The district was appy. Balabitene, since the hereditary
    tenure of the other satrapies was abolished by Zeno (Proc., Aed., III, 1). The 5 satrapies might be
    described as either Syria or Armenia, and perh. this is what J. means. Cf. p. 294.
