  <bloco tipo="nota" script="latino" bbox="200,710,950,790">
    1. Sc. his or her. — 2. The convent was in Sycae (ch. 37), i. e. the suburb beyond the Golden
    Horn. Pargoire (Izvestiya Russkago Arkheologicheskago Istituta v Konstantinopole, IX, p. 261, (1904))
    has shown that the palace of S. Mama was on the Thracian coast opposite Chrysopolis, and the
    monastery of S. Mama at Belgrad Kapu in the SW. of the city; and 'beyond the water' shows the
    palace to be here meant, even if we did not know the site to be in Sycae. V. D. and L.'s rendering
    'ecclesia' in ch. 37 is erroneous, the Syr. having 'house' only.
