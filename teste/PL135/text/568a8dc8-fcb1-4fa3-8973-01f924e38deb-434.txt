  <bloco tipo="texto_principal" script="latino" bbox="100,690,480,930">Italicam Christina Tyrum Vulsinamque comens

Verlit, comminuit, spargit, largitur egenis,

At Christo fugiens attingere membra dicata
Erumpensque globis oleo furit excita turbis
Fiamma, truces spectatores sævasque catervas
Involvens, fervore necans, plexuque perurens.
