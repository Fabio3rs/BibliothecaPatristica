Hear also, my brethren, and beloved this great narrative. There was a woman, dissolute and * vicious of life, married to a man, and she hated him. And after some time, as she was in infidelity and wickedness, she went out of her house one day very late at night before the sun rose, and went to the monastery of the holy and glorious Cross, which a woman may not enter or pass through. When that woman reached it she began to shout, and the wanton made much clamour saying: "O father, do me an act of charity and give heed to hear the statement of my wrong."
