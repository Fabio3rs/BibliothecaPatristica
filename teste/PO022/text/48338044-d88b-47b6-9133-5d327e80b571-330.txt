  <bloco tipo="texto_principal" script="misto" bbox="100,540,930,640">
    The orthography of C. differs from that of the modern hand of A., ٰ is
    very seldom with the diacritical point, the dots below ي are generally written,
    and similarly the points over ة. This orthography is reproduced below :
    where A. gives قَائِلًا and C. قايلا this is not treated as a variant, but when
    قايلا, فِي etc. are cited in variants they are written as in C.
  </bloco>
  <bloco tipo="texto_principal" script="latino" bbox="100,645,930,770">
    The Bohairic Text is nearer to A. but much briefer. It contains 27 out
    of the 56 wonders related in A. and these are for the most part in the same
    order, thus 1, 2, 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 5, 6, 7, 8, 14, 15, 30, 33, 16, 20, 22, 23,
    24, 27, 31, 17, 32, 35, 48, 53. It contains nothing not also found in A. but
    has several items not found in C., viz. 2, 10, 13, 6, 30, 33, 16, 22, 24, 27,
    31, 35, and of these 6, 16, 22, 24, 27, 31 occur also in the Sa'idic.
  </bloco>
  <bloco tipo="texto_principal" script="latino" bbox="100,775,930,960">
    The Sa'idic Text is nearest to A. both in its material and in the order of
    its arrangement, thus 4, 5, 1, 6, 7, 8, 14, 15, 16, 17, 20, 21, 22, 23, 24, 25,
    27, 31, 48, 49, 51. Of these 4, 21, 25, 49, 51 do not occur in C. or B. :
    6, 16, 22, 24, 27, 31 are also in B, but not in C. In no case does matter
    not in B. occur in both C. and S. The Sa'idic is interpolated with a great
    deal of homiletical matter, e.g. 29.a-31.b. story of Jacob, 37.a-38.b com-
    parison to Moses, etc.
  </bloco>
