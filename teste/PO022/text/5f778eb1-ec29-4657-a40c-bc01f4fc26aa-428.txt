    says: 'Thou hast made the Most High a refuge for thee, and no evil shall

    his angels charge over thee and thou shalt trample on the asp and the
    basilisk, and tread upon the lion and the serpent: because he has relied on
    me and I shall deliver him'. » May the merciful Lord God protect us and save
