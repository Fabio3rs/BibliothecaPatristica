And they took hold of him and entreated him. Now at that time there was a monk with him, and there was also the priest Musas, head of the monastery of Anba Samuel which was known as the monastery of as-Sunad, and they said to him: "O holy father Anba Pisentius, we have come to thee that thou mayest do us good and sign us with the sign, do not be annoyed thereby, as the Spirit of God inspires thee." He said to them: "From what district are you?" They said to him: "These two are from the neighbourhood of Aswan, and the two others are of the people of Safat Rashin.
