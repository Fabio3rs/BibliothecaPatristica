  <bloco tipo="texto_principal" script="latino" bbox="100,310,850,335">
    The life of St. Pisentius exists in five recensions :
  </bloco>
  <bloco tipo="texto_principal" script="latino" bbox="100,335,900,410">
    (A.) — An Arabic version in Paris Bib. Nat., Arabe 4785 which was
    copied for Amélineau from a source unknown. As being the fullest narra-
    tive it is the text given below, according to folios 97 to 215 of Amélineau's
    manuscript.
  </bloco>
  <bloco tipo="texto_principal" script="latino" bbox="100,410,900,475">
    (C.) — An Arabic version in Paris Bib. Nat., Arabe 4794, earlier and
    briefer than the preceding. This, numbered in folios 122 b. to 163 b. is given
    below in its variants from the preceding.
  </bloco>
  <bloco tipo="texto_principal" script="latino" bbox="100,475,900,575">
    (B.) — A Bohairic text published by Amélineau (Un évêque de Keft au
    VIIIe siècle, in Mém. prés. et lus à l'Institut égypt., II. (1889), introd. 261-332,
    text and trans. 333-423). This has two numberings, ff. 124-159, and also
    pp. 1-71, where p. 1 = to 124 a, p. 2 = fo 124-b, etc. The page numerals
    are cited below in noting parallel passages.
  </bloco>
  <bloco tipo="texto_principal" script="latino" bbox="100,575,900,645">
    (S.) — A Sa'idic version published by E. A. Wallis Budge (Coptic Apo-
    crypha in the Dialect of Upper Egypt, Lond. (1913), — text 75-127, trans. 258-
    391), from the manuscript Brit. Mus., or. 7024 of date A. D. 985.
  </bloco>
  <bloco tipo="texto_principal" script="latino" bbox="100,645,900,700">
    (D.) — An Arabic version in Paris Bib. Nat., Arabe 478 b, but as this
    appears to be only a second copy of (A) above it does not need further
    attention.
  </bloco>
  <bloco tipo="texto_principal" script="latino" bbox="100,700,900,845">
    The four first named contain a considerable amount of common material,
    most of which appears in its fullest form in A., though S. interpolates a
    number of scriptural illustrations and other matter of a homiletic character.
    In A., after the introduction (97a-104a), the incidents are arranged in 55
    « Wonders » numbered 2 to 56 (there is no number 26). In all the versions
    the matter is set forth in the form of a homily to be read on the saint's
    festival, 13 Abib (= 7 July).
  </bloco>
