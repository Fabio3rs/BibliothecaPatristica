    102 — 1 — V. — « In the midst of the church will I praise thee » 1. —
    When 2 the festival was at its midst, the mediator of God and men, Jesus
    who is God, when standing at that time in the midst of the Jews, showed
    plainly that he is God of God 3 by nature, even after he had emptied himself
    and by dispensation become truly 4 man; and as a partner and sharer of the
    essence (οὐσία) of his 5 invisible Father he 6 says, « I know him, because I am
    from him », and he immediately added further as a mere man and said, « for
    he also sent me ». Therefore let us praise him who through the mediation
    of his mercifulness has joined us to his Godhead 7, according to the riches of
    his great mercy.
