  <bloco tipo="texto_principal" script="latino" bbox="30,600,850,820">
    103 — I — IV. — « Be thou exalted, Lord, in thy might »¹. — Thou, God,
    who bowedest² the heavens and camest down like³ rain⁴ upon a fleece, and
    becamest incarnate⁵ without variation⁶ of the Holy Spirit and of the God-
    bearer the Virgin Mary, and didst fulfil the dispensation in the flesh without
    tumult, didst ascend⁷ in glory to heaven with a shout of God-befitting praise
    and with the voice of a trumpet, being recognised by the bodiless hosts of
    heavenly beings to be King⁸ of Glory, though⁹ thou madest no addition at all
    to the Trinity, and¹⁰ didst remain God, and, being the same, becamest man for
    our sake, who didst raise and set us together¹⁰ with thee in glory in heaven,
    praise to thee!
  </bloco>
