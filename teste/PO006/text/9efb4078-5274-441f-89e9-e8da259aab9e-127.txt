    73 — III — VII. — « Thou hast given me the shield of salvation »¹.
    — Thy Cross, O Christ! contains the whole great mystery of the knowledge
    concerning thee who art God². For, when we ʻsign ourselves³ with the mark
    of its seal, we stretch out and have upon ourselves thee who wast extended
    and hanged upon it of thine own will, but art in all things and hast ʻfilled all
    quarters⁴, and thus are preserved. It blesses and hallows the water of the
    laver of regeneration, and completes the rational, bloodless, spiritual sacrifice,
    and the whole ministerial⁵ office and sacred⁶ service. Thou⁷ who wilt come
    at the end with glory to judge the quick and the dead shinest resplendently in
    heaven, and appearest to all men⁸ in glory. Wherefore we beg of thee,
    strengthen and arm us with it and prepare us to⁹ stand before the dread
    judgment-court, and have mercy upon us.
