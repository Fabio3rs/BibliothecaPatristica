Nunc monstrat cuculus vernalis lumina Phœbi,
Balbula vox resonat gutture raucisono.
Nunc pluvian pendens ramis vocat improba cornix,
Se fallax vitas jactat habere novem.
Vox merulæ taceat, quatit anser plectra palustris,
Vertice submerso sordida quæque vorans.
Vernalis sileat dulces acredula cantus,
Nocturnos reboant bubo inimica sonos.
