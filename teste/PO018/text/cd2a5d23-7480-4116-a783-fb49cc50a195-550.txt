    first, since we were ourselves going up from Alexandria to the royal city ¹,
    he returned with us. And so, when we had travelled a day's journey
    (μακσίων). at every lodging-place, he would send envoys taken from the
    believers to go among them, and ² at every place we used to find from a
    hundred and fifty and from two hundred assembled waiting for him. And
    thus we passed through the cities and villages together; so that in the city
    of Tralles inside the church, while the clergy were performing the service,
    he made more than fifty priests. Since we were a large party, and there were
    distinguished gentlemen among us, we were given permission to occupy an
    upper space, in the upper catechumenia ³ of the church; and so they would come
    up there three and five at a time, while men stood at the doors and kept watch,
    and men who arranged these and sent them on, and this while I myself was
    carrying the oblation and the sacrament ⁴, and he was standing and blessing
    and administering the oblation and dismissing them, and those below were
    performing the service; and I was amazed at the man's courage and fortitude.
