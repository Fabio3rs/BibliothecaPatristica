    great boldness (παρρησία) showing keen enthusiasm and confidence that contends
    with death. When he had stood up and become openly known, envy like
    Gain's was sown in the hearts of many who were inside and not outside; and

    mouth, and you are causing him to rend us all and cast us away in one hour ».
