    whom was a firm faith in the father patriarch, stood up and began to beg

    he answered in a low voice and said to that bishop : « The holy James of
    the church of our father, Saint Macarius, is the man adorned by his deeds ».
    And he commanded the bishop not to disclose this secret to anyone, and
    made him swear this for the sake of the Church, that they might see the
    glory of God perfected in him. After that the holy father died, as we have
    before related. O the great glory of the throne of Saint Mark the evan-

    of them. As the Lord said to his disciples and apostles 1 : « Behold, I am
