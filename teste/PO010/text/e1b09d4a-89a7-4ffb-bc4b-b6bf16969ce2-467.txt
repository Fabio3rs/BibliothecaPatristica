    churches, and burnt the cells with fire. So when the monks came together
    again, they praised the Lord for the renewal of his favour upon them, and
    glorified God for these mercies. The father also rejoiced, seeing that the
    doves had returned to their former nest. In the days of his priesthood he
    had begun to build a sanctuary in the name of Saint Sinuthius, to the
    south of the sanctuary of Saint Macarius; and there the monks began to
    assemble instead of the ruined churches. Now he finished it, and restored
    the other churches. And they praised and glorified the Trinity, as the
    angels do.
