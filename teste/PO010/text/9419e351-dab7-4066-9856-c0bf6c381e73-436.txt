    Now my lords and fathers, and ye children of the orthodox, I told you
    at the beginning that I could never give a perfect account of the deeds of my
    fathers, as the greatness of those deeds deserves; for I am like the gleaner
    after the reapers. And I have no illuminating wit, because I am plunged
    in the darkness of my sins. Yet from reliable and truthful persons, who
    were constantly ministering to our holy fathers, we have heard the nar-
    ratives which we are relating in part. I will therefore give a further
    description of that which was manifested by this father, the patriarch Abba
    Mark, that you may marvel and glorify God for the mercy and compassion
    which he shows to his elect. As Paul says : « For those that love God
    he does every excellent work ».
