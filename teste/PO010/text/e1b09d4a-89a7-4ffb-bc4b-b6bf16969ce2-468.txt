put him to death in retaliation for the murder. But the father strove hard
to save the life of the deacon, whom he had brought up from his youth, and
had intented to promote to a higher degree among the clergy. Thereupon
the Madlajites, seeing that the father was greatly interested in that deacon,
and would not give him up to them, grew violent in their demand, and
required a large sum of money. But the patriarch had nothing with which
to satisfy them, and therefore the bishops and the God-loving people assisted
him, and contributed a sum of money, and so saved him from the hands of
that tribe.
