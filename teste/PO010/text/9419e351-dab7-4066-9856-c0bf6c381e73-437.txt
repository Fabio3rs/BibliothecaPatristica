    in ancient times, and drive away the locusts from the land of Egypt, so hear
    our request to-day, and look upon the tears of thy people and their sighs
    which come from the depth of their hearts, and remove this wrath from
    us ». If we did not abhor prolixity, we would fully report all the prayers

    weeping. And God is the Mighty one, who hears the prayers of his
