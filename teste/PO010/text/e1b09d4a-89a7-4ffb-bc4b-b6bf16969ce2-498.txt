with the holy teachings of the monks, and blessed him by night and by
day, especially on seeing his modesty. Then Joseph was counted worthy
to be made a deacon, and shortly afterwards to be ordained priest by the
father, Abba Mark, the patriarch. As the hegumen Paul grew old, he fell
sick of various diseases, and Joseph, the young saint, served him day and
