    Muslim, whom we have already mentioned in the eighteenth chapter of
    the History of the Church. He was the first who took possession of
    Khorassan. He had married a chaste wife while he was at Harran before
    he began to reign; and afterwards, when he became caliph, he lived at
    Damascus. This woman feared God; and she was of high rank in her
    tribe and nation; and she had made her husband swear, when she was
    married to him, that he would marry no other wife while she lived, so that
    she might keep the Law of God. But when he married her, God gave him no
    child for many years. Afterwards, however, she saw in a dream one who
