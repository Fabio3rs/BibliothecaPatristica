they concerned themselves with these things. And so in every generation
God has not left us without a record. Thus there was the archdeacon,
the spiritual parent of our father, the holy father Abba Cosmas, patriarch
of Alexandria, who was his kinsman. And Abba Macarius also, and
Macarius the monk. And after them John, the spiritual son of Abba Moses,
bishop of Wasim.
And I, poor sinner, was ordered by my father the monk through a dream
which he saw, for he was a holy old man; and he bade me and commanded
me to write the history of my blessed fathers, both what I had witnessed
and what was reported to me by trustworthy persons. For I was minister-
ing to my father Abba Joseph, and slept at his feet; and he was my
spiritual father and was advanced in age. And likewise the father
patriarch, Abba Sinuthius, bade me write. So I prayed to the gracious God,
and said like David : « O Lord open thou my lips, that I may relate
that which happened to the blessed fathers with profit to those that shall
read it, and with benefit to those that shall hear it. »
