    purpose for which I am come ». On hearing this the patriarch said within
    himself¹ : « The pains of death have surrounded me, and the terrors of
    Hell have fallen upon me ». This he said because he knew that there was
    nothing in the churches such as Peter demanded of him. For notorious
    misfortunes had happened to the fathers before Abba Mennas, and none of
    the vessels of the churches had been left among their possessions; for the
    Copts had been robbed of every thing time after time by the adversaries
    who hated them. And when this glorious father was consecrated and
    enthroned, nothing had been restored, so that in the city of Alexandria no
    vessel was found, from which the people could receive the Communion,
    except a chalice of glass and another of wood. Therefore the holy father
    answered that miscreant, saying : « Thou knowest not the condition of the
    church from that time till now ». But the wretched infidel replied : « Lo,
