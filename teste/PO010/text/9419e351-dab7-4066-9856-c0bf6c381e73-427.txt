    When the two men heard these words of the patriarch, they replied with
    humility, through the grace which had come to them by the prayers of

    worthy to be in office nor to remain in it. But we came to thee that we

    we beg of thee, that thou wilt pray the Lord to forgive us the offence of
    our former error and negligence. » This they said in the power of faith

    they sat before him; and they wrote in their own handwriting a declaration
