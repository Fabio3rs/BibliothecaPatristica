Abba Michael, bishop of Misr, advising him to repair to Misr after the holy
feast of Easter, in order to pay his respects to the governor. This was
a dispensation from God, because some of the churches were in a state of
ruin up to that time, and therefore the people mourned.
Accordingly, when the feast of Easter was over, the father patriarch,
Abba Mark, made his entry into Fustat Misr, that he might salute the governor.
And when he reached the city, its bishop, the father Michael,
and the people were informed of their patriarch's arrival, and went out to
him, carrying books of the gospel and crosses and censers, and met him
with great rejoicing and shouting and chanting; and they said : « Happy
and blessed is thy arrival amongst us, O Mark, son of Mark. » Then he
proceeded to his lodging to rest, for it was the end of the day. And on
the morrow the patriarch and the bishop Michael, and the rest of the bishops
assembled with them, arose, that they might pay a visit to the governor.
