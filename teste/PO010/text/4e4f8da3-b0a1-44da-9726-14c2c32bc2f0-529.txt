    And in those days, while the heretic Lazarus was at Alexandria, the
    words that are¹ written were fulfilled concerning him : « Who is he that
    resists the Lord? For he shall be crushed. » For the Lord struck him spee-
    dily with a plague in the following manner. His body and his inward parts
    were swollen with the disease called the dropsy, and the colour of his face
    was changed, and he remained in a recumbent posture, unable to raise his
    head. He had also fallen into great poverty, so that he found none to
    supply nourishment for himself or his beasts, nor to treat him medically.
    Therefore he begged and implored the father patriarch to give him something
    to spend upon himself. And this father did what Lazarus asked, according
    to that which is written², namely : « If thine enemy hunger feed him, and
    if he thirst give him to drink ». And the pains grew upon Lazarus greatly,
