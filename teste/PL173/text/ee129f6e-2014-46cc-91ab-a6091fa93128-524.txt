(50) Ignatius Florentinus, monachus monasterii
Florentiæ, et abbas Casinensis, nobilitate insignis,
eligendo summo pontifice ferebant, quibus peractis

bat, duce Raynerio Gallo, civilitatis portis obsersales,

die Pentecostes incluserunt; siquæ conclavis origi-
